PRIORITY 1 — NAVIGATION AND ONBOARDING
Walk the site as a first-time learner. Can a person immediately understand how to enroll, start, continue, explore modules, see remaining work, and resume? Does the overall training progress bar accurately count the six chapters, chapter capstone, three consequence cases, staffing exercise, tuition planner, evidence journal, and assessment? Does progress persist after refresh, switching tabs, using a separate case window, and returning later? Does changing budget data erroneously count as completing staffing? Are Continue buttons visible, enabled appropriately, and focused correctly? Find dead ends, confusing wording, duplicated menus, inaccessible controls, unwanted scroll jumps, and extraneous text.

PRIORITY 2 — REAL VISUAL AND MOBILE REVIEW

PRIORITY 3 — BUDGET AND TUITION MATHEMATICS
Test js/budget-planner.js from the source, not simply by reading the displayed sample totals:
- Verify tuition and respite revenue, part-time attendance, overnight-only children, overnight nights/stays, and supplies.
- Verify teacher FTE wages, aide FTE wages, payroll load, extra overnight hours, and DIRECTOR SALARY as three clearly separate concepts.
- The director's desired salary is an operating expense, not teacher/aide payroll and NOT the target business profit. Raising it $1,000 must lower monthly surplus $1,000 and raise required tuition appropriately.
- Desired operating profit is after director pay. Verify zero-enrollment and extreme-input handling, currency and negative values, real-vs-example scenario isolation, and effect of resetting examples.
- Test county rent selection for all 58 California counties. Verify published region rate versus statewide fallback, unit conversions for annual/monthly retail asking rents, source labels, data period, and override via actual monthly rent or square-foot footage slider and custom rate.
- Source records are published-market PROXIES, not a true child care property or official statewide average. The fallback is an unweighted average of eight regional benchmarks and must be recomputed if regional figures change. The site fetches the repository's dated JSON when loaded; this is not proof that it automatically crawls market publishers.
- Test NNN/CAM, insurance, utilities percentage of rent, food, incidentals and director compensation separately. Identify omissions like taxes, debt service, vacancies and payroll scheduling.
- Independently calculate monthly break-even enrollment, tuition needed for the profit target, and capacity infeasibility. Challenge every fixed-cost assumption as enrollment increases.
- Test a 24/7 operator scenario with children who attend only overnight. Do not equate sleeping children with automatically permitted sleeping supervision. Check family child care home vs child care center law and whether paid qualified overnight staffing is actually budgeted.
- Evaluate whether the financial outputs are understandable to a director who has never studied accounting.
