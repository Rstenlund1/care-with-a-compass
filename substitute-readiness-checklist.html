Substitute Readiness Checklist

A practical planning aid for California child care homes and centers · v10.9.0 · November 2026

License type comes first. A family child care home licensee’s temporary absence is governed by specific rules, including HSC §1597.63. A child care center operates under different staffing and personnel requirements. A background check, foster approval, school substitute credential or Facebook recommendation does not, by itself, authorize someone to fill every child care role.
